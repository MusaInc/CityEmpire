## WorldState street and retail materials

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The PNGs in this directory are the original colour studies. The game uses the channel-packed copies in `../../pbr`. The runtime pack normalises luminance, blends the tile boundaries, derives small relief normals from luminance and assigns material-specific roughness.

- `worldstate_street_asphalt.png`

  > Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square game environment material albedo tile for WorldState. Weathered but maintained dark charcoal British urban road asphalt. Closely packed angular 4–9 mm grey to black aggregate partially buried in dark tar, faint rolling wear, non-uniform pebble colours. A 1.5 metre square sample. No long cracks, lane markings, puddles, leaves or distinct patches. Exact orthographic top down flat view, macro product scan, perfectly uniform diffuse cross-polarized white lighting. Single material touching all four borders. Make the texture tileable on both axes with matching opposite edges and no repeating feature that would catch the eye. Do not bake directional shading, camera perspective, sheen, cast shadows, specular highlight, vignette, border, background, watermark, labels or other render passes.
